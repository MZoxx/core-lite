## 🐺 WolfPack Staking — Simple Guide

---

### What is it?
Stake your **WP tokens** to earn passive rewards every week.
The reward pool pays out **~1,923,076 WP per epoch** to all stakers — proportional to how much you staked.

---

### 📥 How to Stake

**1.** Go to **QX** and call:
`TransferShareManagementRights → asset: WP, shares: 100, newMgmtIdx: 27`
> This hands your WP tokens over to the WP contract to manage.

**2.** Then on the **WP contract** call:
`Stake → numberOfShares: 100`
> Done! Your tokens are now staking.

---

### 💰 Getting Your Rewards

Rewards are **calculated automatically** at the start of every epoch (weekly).
To actually receive them, call:
`ClaimStakingRewards`
> Your WP tokens land in your wallet. A 100 QU fee is charged by QX.

---

### 🔓 How to Unstake

**1.** Call: `RequestUnstake → numberOfShares: 100`
> Your tokens stop earning rewards immediately.

**2.** Wait **2 epochs**.

**3.** Call: `FinalizeUnstake`
> Your WP tokens are back under QX management (free to trade again). 100 QU fee.

---

### ⚠️ Important

- Always transfer management rights on **QX first** before staking
- You can't stake while an unstake is in progress
- Unstake has a **2-epoch cooldown** — no exceptions
- Every claim and unstake costs **100 QU** (QX network fee)

---

### 📊 Numbers at a Glance

```
Reward per epoch   →  1,923,076 WP
Total staking pool →  ~100,000,000 WP
Duration           →  52 epochs (~1 year)
Unstake delay      →  2 epochs
Fee per action     →  100 QU
```

---

### 🗓️ Example Timeline

```
Epoch 1   →  Transfer rights on QX + Stake 100 WP
Epoch 2   →  Auto-payout: you receive ~10% of 1,923,076 WP (if you hold 10% of staked supply)
Epoch 2   →  Call ClaimStakingRewards → tokens in your wallet
Epoch 5   →  RequestUnstake
Epoch 7   →  FinalizeUnstake → tokens free again
```
